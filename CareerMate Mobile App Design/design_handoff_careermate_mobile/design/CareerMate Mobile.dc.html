Design notes

Gmail's mechanics, Modernist's face

The mail metaphor is kept because the job is sending: one thread per company, a draft that becomes a sent message. CareerMate never reads the mailbox. Whether a company replied is a state you set by hand, on the row or in the thread. What changes is the skin: no pills, no circles, no soft cards. Rows are separated by hairlines, sections by 2px rules, and the accent, now LinkedIn blue rather than the system's red, is spent only on the primary action, the match number and the poster screens.

Monograms are squares, not avatars, and they double as the multi-select target, so selection never needs a long-press the user can't discover.

Patterns borrowed
SearchPinned bar carrying the hamburger and account square
RowsUnread in Archivo 800, read drops to 400
SwipeDrag a row to archive left or mark replied right
FABTwo-way, screenshot capture or manual mail
DrawerStatus labels replace Gmail folders
ThreadPosting, AI draft, sent, plus a manual reply switch
Added to your wireframe

Your four screens are all here. Three more sit behind them: the match breakdown that explains the number, an approval list for AI edits to the CV, and the profile that gives every draft its standing context. Bracketed text in the mail draft is kept exactly as you specified.

Open for you to decide

Corner softness is a tweak: Sharp is the design system's own 0px, Soft (5px, the current default) takes the edge off, Rounded (12px) goes fully Material. Row density, the device shell and match-score visibility sit in the same panel. Two bordered groups on the Add job and CV detail screens were dropped to single rules, so there are fewer boxes for the same structure.
